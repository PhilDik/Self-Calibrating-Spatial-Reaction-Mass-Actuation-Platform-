# Publication note

This repository records an unvalidated technical concept. The current candidate uses four independently controlled solid movers in spatially offset, interwoven closed guide paths. The earlier one-mover testbed and three-ring ground demonstrator are separate embodiments. Compensation, three-axis torque authority and hopping remain unverified.

The corrected working draft incorporates later author discussions. Historical draft dates are retained as historical labels, not as a new release or a verified patent priority date. No novelty or patentability conclusion is asserted.
