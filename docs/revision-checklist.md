# Publication correction checklist

This checklist records documentation corrections. It does not certify hardware validation.

- The current proposal is four separate, spatially offset, interwoven solid-mover paths.
- The central four-channel node is historical and is not part of the current specification.
- One-mover bench and three-ring ground embodiments are identified separately.
- Solid movers are retained; no liquid/plasma or magnetic-suspension redesign is introduced.
- Unbuilt hardware and illustrative numerical targets are labelled explicitly.
- Full-system conservation, frame assumptions, simultaneous loads and integrated impulses are distinguished.
- Three-axis torque authority, force cancellation and full-wrench control are not assumed from mover count.
- Recovery reactions and external contact are included in ground-motion and hop claims.
- Detection/slip thresholds are not described as safety limits.
- Calibration, sensing and degraded-mode authority require validation.
- Closest multi-mass, curved-actuator and adaptive-control prior art is included with limits.
- Cui/Lu/Zhu replaces the incorrect Liu attribution; the unverified video is not technical evidence.
- Philipp Dik and the actual repository URL appear in active metadata.
- No new licence, patent-pending claim, DOI or release is invented.
- Existing figures are marked as conceptual testbed/historical drawings; no unverified four-path CAD is supplied.
