# Prior-art context

**Review completed:** 2026-09-20  
**Status:** bounded literature/patent review; no conclusion of patentability or freedom to operate.

## Closest identified work

- [US20120232780A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20120232780A1/en), published 2012-09-13; [US8981682B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8981682B2/en), granted 2015-03-17. Describes synchronized vibration actuators, including a four-ERM tetrahedral arrangement and phase-dependent force/torque effects. Generic four-mass spatial coordination and acceleration/braking are not an established novelty boundary. The disclosed rotors are not the proposed four non-planar guide paths.
- [EP0505976A1 / EP0505976B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP0505976B1/en), A1 published 1992-09-30, B1 granted 1994-09-21. Four independently driven eccentric masses with adaptive vibration/force/couple compensation. The parallel-axis arrangement differs from the proposed spatial guides.
- [WO2016131032A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2016131032A1/en) and [WO2016131033A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2016131033A1/en), published 2016-08-18; [US11152882B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11152882B2/en), granted 2021-10-19. Curved reciprocating reaction masses, feedback and response characterization/control tables are relevant to both geometry and calibration. Reciprocal arcs differ from continuous closed-loop circulation.
- [Markley et al., Maximum Torque and Momentum Envelopes for Reaction Wheel Arrays, NASA NTRS 20110015369](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110015369). Established bounded torque/momentum allocation for multi-wheel arrays. Four spatially oriented rotating masses for three-axis control are not new; their fixed-axis wheel mechanics differ from the proposed circulating guide movers.
- [Shimoda and colleagues, Mobility of a microgravity rover using internal electro-magnetic levitation, IROS 2000, DOI 10.1109/IROS.2000.895207](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS.2000.895207). Internal iron-ball actuation and hopping/reaction motion are prior experimental subjects. This levitation system is an adjacent mechanism, not the proposed guided four-loop architecture.

## Existing sources and their limits

1. Cui, Lu and Zhu, [Two-dimensional point suspension characteristics of a magnetic robot driven by dual electromagnets within a fluid pipe](https://ms.copernicus.org/articles/17/825/2026/), Mechanical Sciences 17 (2026), DOI 10.5194/ms-17-825-2026, published 2026-08-25. Corrects the earlier attribution to Liu. It studies a magnetized NdFeB sphere suspended in fluid; it does not demonstrate a rolling plain-steel reaction mass or the proposed platform.
2. Tafrishi et al., [Design, Modeling and Motion Analysis of a Novel Fluid Actuated Spherical Rolling Robot](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4043689), Journal of Mechanisms and Robotics 11(4), 2019. Guided moving masses and fluid-actuated spherical locomotion; model/design evidence, not demonstration of the proposed electromagnetic drive.
3. [Inverse Dynamics-Based Motion Control of a Fluid-Actuated Rolling Robot](https://doi.org/10.20537/nd190420), 2019. Related model and control study; not validation of the current four-path architecture.
4. [CN110979500A](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN110979500A/en). Fluid-driven spherical rolling robot with guided mass/centre-of-gravity adjustment. Mechanically relevant; no claim that patent publication verifies performance.
5. [WO2018002555A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2018002555A1/en). Closed-guide circulating mass with controlled braking and optional electromagnetic drive. Relevant as disclosed topology only. Its proposed reaction-free return braking is not accepted as evidence of reactionless propulsion.
6. [US20080168862A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20080168862A1/en). Variable-radius rotating masses and guide geometry; different from four interwoven closed paths.
7. [US9479698B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9479698B2/en). Surface movement using an internal vibration motor; external contact is essential to the comparison.
8. [US10857670B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10857670B2/en), granted 2020-12-08; [US20160325429A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20160325429A1/en), published 2016-11-10. Modular robots using rapid internal flywheel braking for torque impulses and contact-mediated motion; not continuous guideway movers.
9. Korendiy and Kachur, [Frontiers in Robotics and AI, 2023, DOI 10.3389/frobt.2023.1239137](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2023.1239137/full). Vibration-driven surface locomotion; does not validate this architecture.
10. [Locomotion and Control of a Friction-Driven Tripedal Robot, arXiv:2011.07370](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.07370). Surface/friction control with a different mechanism. Cite the inspected preprint as such.
11. [The Wheelbot, arXiv:2207.06988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.06988). Reaction-wheel self-erection and balancing; a benchmark for known momentum-exchange principles, not the proposed guide actuation.

The previously linked six-coil YouTube video R1Dlyg4FFZM could not be independently verified and is omitted as technical evidence.

## Commercial and adjacent systems

[Accretech SBS automatic balancing](https://accretechsbs.com/what-we-do/) uses vibration sensing and commanded counterweights. [DEICON's proof-mass control case study](https://deicon.com/resource/active-vibration-control-using-proof-mass-actuators/) describes feedback vibration suppression. These show that sensing and adaptive internal-mass control are established engineering, not a new general principle.

## Limits of the comparison

The specific candidate remains four spatially offset, interwoven, oppositely twisted solid-mass paths with phase-dependent actuation. The search did not establish an exact equivalent, but this is not evidence of novelty. No dimensioned geometry, validated control model or performance demonstration is supplied.

Patent descriptions disclose mechanisms; they do not certify working physics. Technical disclosure, independent claim scope and present legal enforceability are different questions. Official register access was incomplete; no legal-status conclusion is made. Multiple sources or separate embodiments must not be silently combined into a single anticipation finding.
